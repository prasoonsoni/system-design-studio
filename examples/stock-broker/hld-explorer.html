Stock Broker Trading Platform

A retail brokerage sits between the user and the exchange — it never matches orders itself. Its job is auth, funds, risk, routing, market data and settlement around the exchange's matching engine. Pick a view, then tap any block to see what it does and how it works.

Follow a single buy order from tap to settlement. The broker validates and risk-checks it, routes it to the exchange over FIX, then fans the result out through the event bus. Tap a step for detail.

    Scope. This is an HLD — component responsibilities, data flow and mechanics, not line-level implementation. The money and order paths favour strong consistency (CP); market data and watchlists favour availability (AP). Reconciliation with the exchange closes the loop each day.
